Concentration cap BOUND The 25% per-provider cap bound on this print; it is mathematically inert at n=4 and binds only from n=5.

How the number is made

Three commitments, each one checkable against the repository rather than taken on trust.

Full methodology

Reproducible

Every parameter is a visible config value, and the five files that can change a print are hash-locked. Every print can be rebuilt from public sources by anyone with the repo.

Vendor-neutral

A weighted median over offers from marketplaces, neoclouds and sovereign operators, gated at 5 providers, with a 25% concentration cap — no single source sets the price.

Open methodology

Design follows the IOSCO Principles for Financial Benchmarks as voluntary best practice — published, versioned, and change-controlled, with the full constituent set behind every print.

A family of indices

One index is published today. The rest are stated as what they are — in development or planned — and carry no numbers until they do.

Live

TCI‑CRI

Compute Reference Index — GPU-hour pricing across 6 hardware classes, published daily with its full constituent set.

In development

TCI‑ERI

Energy Reference Index — the power cost behind the compute cost. Day-ahead power prices are already collected as an overlay; they do not enter any index value.

Planned

TCI‑SRI

Storage Reference Index — reference pricing for high-throughput storage. No data is collected for this yet.

Full history. CSV and JSON.

Every observation, every print, every constituent set — the candidates that were rejected as well as the ones that counted, and the reason for each. Downloadable and versioned since day one.

Important information

TCI is a research publication. It is not investment advice and is not administered as a benchmark under EU Regulation 2016/1011; it may not be used as a reference price in financial instruments.

TCI is a price-transparency benchmark, not a settlement benchmark. It is not transaction-based, is not administered by an authorised benchmark administrator, and must not be referenced in a financial contract. Values are derived from third-party public price surfaces believed to be reliable but are not independently verified; observed prices may differ materially from prices actually obtainable. Past values are not indicative of future values. A session with too few qualifying providers is published as a gap — never back-filled, never carried forward.
